## Overview

The Sales Bench provides enterprise commercial strategy, RevOps architecture, and execution leadership:
1. **Sales Systems & Structure for Founders:** Transitioning out of founder-led selling into a documented, scalable playbook and CRM stage gates (Harvard Business Review benchmark: +18% to 28% higher revenue growth).
2. **Sales Team Productivity & Deal Rigor:** Hard-coded MEDDPICC qualification, Mutual Action Plans (MAPs), and POD alignment (+28% win rate lift, <5% forecast variance).
3. **North America & Cross-Border Expansion:** 4-Week Validation Sprint with live enterprise buyers, localized compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, PIPEDA), USD/CAD pricing, and Day-1 compliant rep onboarding via Employer of Record (G-P).
