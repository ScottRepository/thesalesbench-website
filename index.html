How We Work: 4-Week Sprint to 90–120 Day Execution

No open-ended retainers. Every client engagement begins with a focused 4-Week Commercial Sprint that delivers clarity before rolling into hands-on implementation.

Phase 1 // 4-Week Sprint

The Commercial Validation Sprint

A rapid audit of your commercial motion, target buyer resonance, channel feasibility, and tech stack—delivering actionable clarity in 30 days.

1
Week 1: Commercial Motion Teardown: Pipeline conversion, deal slippage, qualification gates, and ICP criteria.
2
Week 2: Live Market & Partner Discovery: 2–4 discovery conversations with real enterprise buyers and prospective ecosystem partners.
3
Week 3: Tech Stack & RevOps Specification: Audit tool stack integrations and map CRM stage specifications for your internal team.
4
Week 4: 90–120 Day Architecture Blueprint: Final presentation of your commercial roadmap, channel strategy, and board-ready deliverables.

Roll directly from the sprint into collaborative execution—directing your internal RevOps team on configuration while standing up partner alliances.

C

D
Deal Rigor & Forecast Governance: Weekly deal inspections, Mutual Action Plans, and manager coaching to ensure pipeline predictability.
