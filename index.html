The Protocol In Practice

We interrogate the evidence, not the feeling.

When an AE says a six-figure deal is "100% closing this month," we ask:

"Who signed off on the business case at the CFO level, when was the security questionnaire submitted, and who is the procurement contact executing the PO?"

If those answers aren't in the call transcripts or the deal room, the deal is not in commit. That is how we prevent 25%–35% of late-stage slippage.
