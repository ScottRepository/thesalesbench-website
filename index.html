The Anti-Fluff Protocol in Practice

When an AE claims a six-figure enterprise deal is "100% closing this month," we don't ask how the relationship feels. We interrogate the evidence:

"Who signed off on the business case at the CFO level, what date was the security questionnaire submitted to their InfoSec team, and what is the exact name of the procurement contact executing the PO?"
